## Fiscal terminology

Use **إشعار دائن** and **إشعار مدين** for credit/debit notes, and keep the
source invoice reference intact. This follows the terminology used in ZATCA's
e-invoicing and VAT guidance. Use **رقم التسجيل الضريبي** for the VAT
registration number and **ضريبة القيمة المضافة** in user-facing fiscal text.

Sources: [ZATCA simplified credit/debit-note guide](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/HelpCenter/guidelines/Documents/SIMPLIFIED%20FATOORA_guideline_AR.pdf) and [ZATCA VAT guidance](https://zatca.gov.sa/ar/RulesRegulations/VAT/Pages/default.aspx/1000).
